# 中文图注

**图 X｜候选药物筛选、雷公藤甲素与 16-hydroxytriptolide 的 S100A8 比较对接及雷公藤甲素分子动力学分析。**

**A，**原始计算机筛选所得的 18 个候选化合物与 S100A8 的二值关联矩阵；1 和 0 分别表示筛选结果为阳性和阴性，16-OH-TPL（16-hydroxytriptolide）以紫色突出显示。矩阵仅为适应组图而重排为两列，药物身份、顺序和数值均保持不变。
**B，**雷公藤甲素与 16-hydroxytriptolide 的二维结构及分子指纹相似性。
**C，**两种化合物在 S100A8 同源二聚体 BD 界面预测口袋中的代表性对接构象叠合；链 B、链 D、雷公藤甲素和 16-hydroxytriptolide 分别以蓝色、青绿色、橙色和紫色表示。
**D，**共同界面口袋内代表性构象及预测极性相互作用的局部放大图。
**E，**相同受体、评分函数和随机种子条件下，16-hydroxytriptolide 与雷公藤甲素最佳对接评分之差；每个点代表一个随机种子（每种条件 n = 10），黑色横线表示中位数，正值表示 16-hydroxytriptolide 的评分更不负。
**F，**代表性构象的蛋白接触集合 Jaccard 指数；柱上数字为两种化合物 26 个共有重原子骨架的直接 RMSD。
**G，**蛋白主链 RMSD；灰色阴影和 20 ns 竖向虚线标示预平衡区间及后续分析起点。
**H，**S100A8 链 B 与链 D 的质心距离。
**I，**20-100 ns 内按链局部拟合计算的主链 RMSF。
**J，**蛋白回转半径（Rg）。
**K，**蛋白溶剂可及表面积（SASA）。
**L，**两条 S100A8 链之间的氢键数量。G-L 中实线为三个独立 100 ns 重复的逐时点中位数，阴影为三个重复的最小值至最大值范围；红色和蓝色分别表示 S100A8-雷公藤甲素复合物和 apo S100A8。
**M，**蛋白拟合后雷公藤甲素的非周期展开 RMSD。
**N，**雷公藤甲素相对于初始对接口袋的质心位移。
**O，**雷公藤甲素与蛋白的最小距离。
**P，**雷公藤甲素-蛋白接触数。
**Q，**雷公藤甲素-蛋白氢键数。M-Q 分别显示三个独立复合物重复。
**R，**三个复合物重复中占有率最高的 14 个雷公藤甲素-残基接触。
**S，**在三个重复合并建立的共同主成分基底上，复合物与 apo S100A8 的 PC1-PC2 概率密度等高线；实线、虚线和点线分别包围 50%、75% 和 95% 的概率质量，圆形、方形和三角形分别表示重复 1、2 和 3 的质心。
**T，**复合物与 apo S100A8 在同一共同主成分基底上的 300 K 自由能景观；色标为相对自由能 ΔG（kJ mol⁻¹）。所有分子动力学面板均仅针对雷公藤甲素；未对 16-hydroxytriptolide 进行分子动力学模拟或实验验证。

# English figure legend

**Figure X | Candidate-drug screening, comparative docking of triptolide and 16-hydroxytriptolide to S100A8, and molecular dynamics analysis of triptolide.**

**A,** Binary association matrix between S100A8 and the 18 candidates identified by the original computational screen. Values of 1 and 0 denote positive and negative screening results, respectively; 16-OH-TPL (16-hydroxytriptolide) is highlighted in purple. The matrix was rearranged into two columns solely to fit the composite; drug identities, ordering and values were unchanged.
**B,** Two-dimensional structures of triptolide and 16-hydroxytriptolide and their molecular-fingerprint similarities.
**C,** Overlay of representative docking poses in the predicted S100A8 homodimer BD-interface pocket. Chains B and D are shown in blue and teal, respectively; triptolide and 16-hydroxytriptolide are shown in orange and purple, respectively.
**D,** Close-up view of the representative poses and predicted polar interactions in the shared interface pocket.
**E,** Difference between the best docking scores of 16-hydroxytriptolide and triptolide under matched receptor, scoring-function and random-seed conditions. Each point denotes one seed (n = 10 per condition), and black horizontal lines denote medians; positive values indicate a less-negative score for 16-hydroxytriptolide.
**F,** Jaccard indices for protein-contact sets of the representative poses; labels above the bars report direct RMSDs for the 26-heavy-atom common scaffold.
**G,** Protein backbone RMSD; the grey shading and vertical line at 20 ns denote the pre-equilibration interval and the start of the subsequent analysis window.
**H,** Centre-of-mass distance between S100A8 chains B and D.
**I,** Chain-locally fitted backbone RMSF over 20-100 ns.
**J,** Protein radius of gyration (Rg).
**K,** Protein solvent-accessible surface area (SASA).
**L,** Number of inter-chain S100A8 hydrogen bonds. In G-L, solid lines show pointwise medians across three independent 100-ns replicas and shading shows the full replica range; red and blue denote the S100A8-triptolide complex and apo S100A8, respectively.
**M,** Unwrapped triptolide RMSD after protein fitting.
**N,** Triptolide centre-of-mass displacement from the original docking pocket.
**O,** Minimum triptolide-protein distance.
**P,** Number of triptolide-protein contacts.
**Q,** Number of triptolide-protein hydrogen bonds. M-Q show the three independent complex replicas separately.
**R,** The 14 highest-occupancy triptolide-residue contacts across the three complex replicas.
**S,** PC1-PC2 probability-density contours of the complex and apo S100A8 on a pooled principal-component basis constructed from all replicas. Solid, dashed and dotted contours enclose 50%, 75% and 95% probability mass, respectively; circles, squares and triangles denote the centroids of replicas 1, 2 and 3, respectively.
**T,** 300-K free-energy landscapes of the complex and apo S100A8 on the same pooled basis; the colour scale denotes relative free energy ΔG (kJ mol⁻¹). All molecular dynamics panels concern triptolide only; no molecular dynamics simulation or experimental validation of 16-hydroxytriptolide is implied.
